Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

Bench is a personal command center for running a research lab, made by an independent developer. This policy covers the Bench app for Mac and iPhone, and the Bench widgets.

The short version

  • Your data stays on your devices and in your own private iCloud account.
  • Bench has no servers, no accounts and no sign-ups.
  • Bench has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and the developer never sees your data.
  • Bench goes online for a few specific jobs, listed below, and sends only what that job needs.

Where your data lives

Everything you enter in Bench (projects, grants, papers, people, collaborators, orders, deadlines, events, presentations, teaching, Today lists and settings) is stored on your device.

If you’re signed in to iCloud, Bench syncs that data between your Mac and iPhone through your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). Only your Apple Account can read that database. The developer can’t. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

If you’re not signed in to iCloud, your data stays on that one device.

Backups you export from Settings are ordinary files that you save wherever you choose. Bench doesn’t upload them anywhere.

When Bench goes online

Bench connects to these outside services only when you use the feature that needs them:

These services see the request and your device’s IP address, as any website would. Their own privacy policies apply to what they receive. Bench doesn’t send them anything else from your library.

Links you tap in Bench (a DOI, a PubMed page, a meeting link) open in your browser or the relevant app, like any other link.

Permissions

  • Contacts: used only when you choose “Fill from Contacts”. Bench reads the one contact you pick, to fill in a person’s photo and details. It never reads your whole address book.
  • Camera (iPhone): used only by the order barcode scanner. Images are processed on the device and never saved or sent.

What Bench does not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting services, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No sharing or selling of data to anyone.
  • No collecting data from children. Bench is a professional tool for researchers.

Deleting your data

Delete items inside Bench, or delete the app. To remove synced data from iCloud too: on iPhone go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Bench; on Mac go to System Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
