Bench

lightweight project management for research labs

Bench is built around how an academic research lab actually works. Grants, papers, projects, people, service work and teaching live in one calm place, with a short list of what matters today on top. It's native on Mac and iPhone, so use either or both, and it syncs through your own iCloud.

Take the tour ↓ Coming soon to Mac & iPhone

A personal project, built by a working PI. Free. For macOS 26 and iOS 26.

i. Today

Start every morning with a short list

Today gives you three buckets: Top Priority, Secondary Goals and Quick Wins. Anything you don't finish carries over on its own. Each task can link back to the grant, paper or project it belongs to, so you can see why it matters.

  • Deadlines turn red, amber or orange as they get closer
  • Push a task to tomorrow or the next workday with one tap
  • Pin important emails and links right on a task
  • Star up to three things as your Focus Area, and they stay in view underneath

ii. Projects

Every project, and the very next step

See every project in the lab at once. Each card shows the next step, who's on it and how long it's been waiting, so you can tell what's moving and what's stuck without opening anything.

When you need the detail, open a project and it's all there: the question it's asking, its aims laid out as experiments, and the figures, links and people that belong to it.

  • Active, Planning and On Hold lanes, plus an Idea Bin
  • Ideas graduate into projects, question and aims included
  • Give each project its own picture or emoji

iii. Grants

Grants, from first draft to final year

The grant pipeline runs from Pre-Submission to In Review to Funded. Upcoming internal and agency deadlines sit at the top. Open a funded award to see which year it's in, how far through that year you are, and when the next progress report is due. Proposals in review carry their impact score.

  • Study section dates, impact scores and budgets
  • No-cost extensions and award years at a glance
  • Linked emails and websites for every award

iv. Papers

Manuscripts that keep moving

Follow every manuscript from first draft to print. Each card shows how long a paper has been sitting at its stage, so a stalled one is easy to spot, and each paper keeps a timeline of every submission, rejection and resubmission.

Then tap the magic wand. Bench checks PubMed for your papers in review, accepted or just out, and finalizes the ones that have been published, with the full citation filled in and a seal on the card.

  • Author lines, target journals and revision rounds
  • A publication chart of your lab's output over the years
  • Link each paper to the projects and grants it came from

v. People & lab

Your lab, face by face

Everyone in the lab appears with their role, year and what they're working on, and upcoming time away shows at the top before it catches you out. Open a person for their projects, tasks, papers and training records.

The rest of running a lab lives here too. Collaborators sit on a map, ranked by how closely you work together, and lab orders show what's on the way and what's running late.

  • Training records and out-of-office on each person
  • Email anyone in one tap
  • A barcode scanner for stock-takes

vi. Service & admin

Lighten your administrative load

Study sections, peer review, committees, society roles: each role gets its own card, grouped into lanes, so you can see at a glance where your time is going. Busy roles show their next few tasks, and quiet ones shrink to a pill.

Open a role to find its tasks, key dates, important emails and links in one place. Its due dates flow into Today and Deadlines, so a score sheet never sneaks up on you.

  • "Next due" right at the top of every role
  • Recurring key dates that roll forward on their own
  • On the Mac, drag an email straight from Mail onto a role

vii. Deadlines & events

Nothing sneaks up on you

Deadlines are gathered for you from your grants, papers, committees and events, so you never retype a date. Each week is laid out the way you'd plan it, and each item shows where it came from.

Events get a map of where the year takes you: conferences, seminars and study section meetings, each with its dates, your talk and what needs doing before you go.

  • Mark yourself going or maybe, and see how far off it is
  • A CV-style log of every poster and talk, yours and your lab's
  • Invited talks stand out from the rest

viii. Teaching

Courses and guest lectures, without the scramble

Each course shows its next class and whether you've prepped it. Open one for the meeting times, the teaching team, this week's sessions and the key dates for the term. Guest lectures you give in other people's classes are tracked too, each with its own prep list.

  • Course site and Zoom links one tap away
  • Midterms, grade deadlines and other term dates kept with the course
  • Past guest lectures kept by year, ready for your CV

And the small things

Built the way a lab actually runs

☁️ Your iCloud, your data

Everything syncs through your own iCloud account. There are no servers and no sign-up.

💻 Mac and iPhone

Do the heavy data entry on the Mac and tick things off on your phone. Nearly every feature is on both.

🎙️ Siri

Say "Add a Top Priority to Bench" from anywhere. Secondary Goals and Quick Wins work the same way.

🧩 Home Screen widget

Keep today's list on your Home Screen, and tick tasks off right from the widget.

⚡️ Quick actions

Press and hold the app icon to add a task to any of Today's buckets.

🔎 Search everything

One search covers projects, grants, papers, people, events and tasks.
